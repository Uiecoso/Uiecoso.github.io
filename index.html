Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Thank you for using it's fate. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the mobile application published under the bundle identifier com.itsfate.app.

1. Information We Collect

The app is designed so that its core features work without an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information.

Stored locally on your device:

  • Fortune draw results and your card history
  • Activity status for the current day, and your daily draw count
  • Your language preference
  • A randomly generated anonymous device identifier

Sent to our servers, only when verifying a purchase:

  • An anonymous device identifier
  • The transaction identifier and product identifier of your purchase
  • The purchase receipt / transaction payload issued by the platform store

2. Purchase Information

Premium features are sold through the platform's in-app purchase system (the Apple App Store on iOS, Google Play on Android). Payments are processed entirely by the platform; we never receive or store your payment card details.

To confirm that a purchase is valid and to unlock Premium features, the app sends the transaction identifier and receipt to our verification server. Our server forwards this to Apple's App Store Server API for validation and stores the resulting record (anonymous device identifier, transaction identifier, product identifier, platform, and verification timestamp).

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the fortune-drawing features and keep track of your daily progress
  • To verify in-app purchases and unlock Premium features on your device
  • To restore your purchase on the same device or the same store account
  • To remember your language preference
  • To diagnose and fix technical faults

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your usage data — draw results, card history, daily counts, and language preference — is stored locally on your device and can be erased at any time using Settings → Reset All Data.

Purchase verification records are stored on our servers, which are protected by HTTPS in transit and by access controls at rest. These records contain only the anonymous device identifier, transaction identifier, product identifier, platform, and timestamp. They contain no name, email address, or payment details.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the platform's in-app purchase service (Apple App Store / Google Play). Your purchase is also subject to the platform's own privacy policy. We do not integrate advertising networks, attribution SDKs, or third-party analytics that track you across other apps or websites.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on your device until you reset it in Settings or delete the app. Purchase verification records are kept for as long as needed to honour your purchase and to meet accounting and legal obligations. You may request deletion of the verification record associated with your anonymous device identifier by contacting us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to object to its processing. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

9. International Transfers

Our verification server may process data in a country other than yours. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the data continues to receive an adequate level of protection.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page, and where appropriate we will also notify you in the app.
